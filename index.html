Privacy (POPIA)

We collect only what you send us when you contact us, such as your name, cellphone number and details of your request. We use it only to provide the service you asked for. We do not sell it and we do not use it for advertising.

Your messages travel through WhatsApp. We share information with others only when it is needed to run the service (for example a payment provider when you pay) or when the law requires it.

Never send your ID number, bank PIN, card details or SASSA PIN on WhatsApp. We will never ask for them.

You may ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Contact us using the details above. Our Information Officer is Amahle Mtshubungu. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Delivery

All our services are digital. After you message us we reply within 1 business day, agree what is covered and your start date, and send your reports by WhatsApp or email.

Payments, cancelling and refunds
  • Prices are in rand. We confirm the amount and how to pay with you on WhatsApp before you pay anything.
  • The 24/7 Digital Army is paid per month and the FYI Audit per week, in advance. Nothing is charged automatically. To stop, tell us before the next payment is due.
  • You may cancel a new Digital Army or FYI Audit within 7 days of your first payment for a full refund, as long as we have not started. If we have started, we refund the part not yet delivered after a fair charge for work done.
  • Finder tracks that have been used are not refundable. If you have used none, you may ask for a refund within 7 days of paying. Unused tracks do not expire.
  • If you were charged by mistake or twice, contact us. If we confirm it, we refund you within 7 business days.
  • Nothing here limits your rights under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act.

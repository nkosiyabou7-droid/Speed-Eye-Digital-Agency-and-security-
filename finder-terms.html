Owner's Finder: Device-Owner Terms

Speed Eye Digital Agency & Security · Army 4 · Last updated 15 November 2026

These terms apply on top of our general Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

1. What Finder is

Owner's Finder helps you find your own phone if it is lost or stolen. You open the Finder page on that phone and choose to share its location. Later, from any phone or computer, you use your Finder code to see where it was last seen. Each look-up uses one "track".

2. Only your own device

By ticking the agreement box you confirm that:

  • the phone you register belongs to you, and the IMEI you give is its IMEI;
  • you will not register or locate anyone else's phone, and you will not use Finder to watch, follow or monitor another person, including a partner, ex-partner, family member, employee or friend;
  • you are 18 or older.

Using a tracking service on someone else's device without their knowledge and agreement can be a crime in South Africa. If we find or suspect that Finder is being used this way, we may end the service straight away without a refund, and we may report it to the police.

3. Children's phones

Finder is meant for adults' own phones. If you are a parent or legal guardian and want to find a child's phone for the child's safety, you may do so only for a child under your legal care, and the child should be told in a way suited to their age. [Owner to decide whether to allow this.]

4. How sharing works

  • Sharing starts only when you tap Start sharing on the phone and allow location in the browser.
  • A green bar shows on the page for as long as sharing is on. You can tap Stop sharing at any time.
  • Sharing only works while the Finder page stays open on that phone. If the page is closed, the battery dies, or the phone is switched off or has no signal, we cannot see it. Finder is not a hidden app and cannot track a phone in the background.
  • We keep only the latest location the phone shared. Each new location replaces the one before it.
  • We record how many tracks you have used, but not a history of where the phone has been.

4A. Your Finder code

  • After you pay, the page shows you a Finder code. Treat it like a password.
  • Anyone who has your code can look up the phone's last location. Do not share it, and do not post it online.
  • If you lose your code, contact us. We may ask for proof that you are the person who paid, such as your name, email, phone number and payment details.

5. Tracks and prices

  • R5 buys 1 track. R499 buys 100 tracks. All prices are in rand (ZAR). [State whether VAT is included.]
  • A track is used each time a look-up returns a location. If the phone has not shared a location yet, nothing is used.
  • Tracks are not refundable once used. Unused tracks do not expire. If you have used none, you may ask for a refund within 7 days of paying (see our Refund policy).
  • Payments go through Paystack. We never see your card details.

6. What we do not promise

  • We cannot promise that Finder will find your phone, or that a location will be shown, current or accurate. Accuracy depends on the phone, its signal and the browser, and may be out by many metres.
  • Finder is not an emergency or police service. If your phone was stolen or you feel unsafe, report it to the South African Police Service.
  • Do not go to a location yourself to take back a phone. Give the information to the police.
  • To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for loss or harm caused by acting on a location Finder shows.

7. Your information

Finder uses your name, email, cellphone number, the IMEI of your phone, and the latest location your phone shares. We use it only to give you this service. We do not sell it and we do not share it with other customers.

We will give a location to the police or a court only if the law requires it and a proper order or request is made. We delete your Finder information on request, and on ending the service, unless the law requires us to keep it. For your rights under POPIA, see our Privacy Policy.

8. Ending the service

You can stop sharing at any time and ask us to delete your information. We may suspend or end Finder if you break these terms, misuse the service, or if the law requires it.

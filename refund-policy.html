Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy

Speed Eye Digital Agency & Security · Last updated 15 November 2026

1. How our services are delivered

  • All our services are digital. Nothing is posted or couriered.
  • Army 1 (24/7 Digital Army) and Army 2 (FYI Audit): after you pay, we contact you within 1 business day to agree your start date and what is covered. Reports are sent to you by email.
  • Army 3 (Grant Insurer): free for beneficiaries. There is no payment and nothing to refund.
  • Army 4 (Owner's Finder): after you pay, your Finder code appears on the screen straight away and your tracks are ready to use.

2. Cancelling a new service

  • You may cancel Army 1 or Army 2 within 7 days of your first payment, for a full refund, as long as we have not started the service.
  • If we have already started, we refund the part not yet delivered, after taking off a fair charge for work already done.
  • Tell us by email or phone (details below) and give the name and email you paid with.

3. Monthly and weekly services: how to stop

  • Army 1 is paid per month and Army 2 per week, in advance. Your card is not charged automatically. We tell you before each new period is due.
  • To stop, tell us before the next payment is due. After that, you owe nothing more and we do not charge you again.
  • If you have not paid by the due date, we may pause the service until you do.

4. Finder tracks

  • R5 buys 1 track and R499 buys 100 tracks. A track is used each time a look-up shows a location.
  • Used tracks are not refundable.
  • If you have used none, you may ask for a refund within 7 days of paying.
  • Unused tracks do not expire.

5. Wrong or double charges

If you were charged by mistake or twice, contact us with your name, the email you paid with and the payment reference from your receipt. If we confirm the mistake, we refund you within 7 business days, to the same card or account you paid from. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
